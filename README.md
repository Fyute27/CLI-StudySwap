# StudySwap 📚

## Student Marketplace for Used Study Materials

StudySwap is a student-focused website that allows university students to **buy, sell, and exchange used textbooks, notes, and other study materials**.

The platform provides a simple and organized alternative to using Facebook groups or expensive bookstores.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

University textbooks and study materials can be expensive. Many students also have books that they no longer need after completing a semester.

StudySwap connects students who want to:

- 💰 Save money by buying used textbooks
- 📖 Sell textbooks they no longer need
- 🔄 Exchange books and study materials
- 💬 Communicate with other students
- ⭐ Build trust through ratings and reviews

### Problem

Students currently use platforms such as Facebook groups to buy and sell used textbooks. However, these groups can be messy, difficult to search, and inconvenient.

### Solution

StudySwap provides a dedicated platform where university students can easily find, buy, sell, and exchange study materials.

---

## 👥 Target Users

StudySwap is designed mainly for:

- University students
- Year 1–4 students
- Students who want to save money
- Students selling unused textbooks
- Students looking for books from previous semesters

---

## ✨ Main Features

### 👤 User Management

- Student registration
- Login and logout
- User profile
- Student verification

### 📚 Study Material Listings

Students can:

- Create listings
- Add textbook information
- Add descriptions
- Set prices
- Select item condition
- Upload images
- Choose between selling or exchanging
- Edit their listings
- Delete their listings

### 🔎 Search & Filter

Users can find study materials using:

- Keywords
- Course
- Category
- Price
- Condition
- Sale or exchange type

### 💬 Chat System

Buyers and sellers can communicate about:

- Price
- Book condition
- Availability
- Exchange
- Meeting arrangements

### 🔄 Purchase & Exchange

Users can send requests to purchase or exchange study materials.

Sellers can:

- Accept requests
- Reject requests
- Update listing status

### ⭐ Rating & Review

After completing a transaction, students can:

- Rate other users
- Write reviews
- View ratings
