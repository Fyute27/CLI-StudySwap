# StudySwap - Project Charter

## 1. Project Title

**StudySwap**

## 2. Project Overview

StudySwap is a student-focused website that allows university students to buy, sell, and exchange used textbooks, notes, and other study materials.

The platform is designed to provide students with a simple, fast, and trustworthy alternative to existing methods such as Facebook groups and bookstores.

StudySwap aims to help students save money while reducing the amount of study material that is left unused after each semester.

## 3. Problem Statement

University students often spend a large amount of money on textbooks and study materials.

After a semester ends, many students no longer need their books, but finding other students who need those materials can be difficult.

Current solutions such as Facebook groups are often messy and difficult to search, while bookstores can be expensive.

Students need a fast and trustworthy way to buy, sell, and exchange used study materials.

## 4. Project Goals

The main goals of StudySwap are:

- Help students save money by buying used study materials.
- Allow students to sell textbooks and notes they no longer need.
- Make it easier to find study materials from previous semesters.
- Provide a simple platform specifically for university students.
- Reduce waste by allowing students to reuse study materials.
- Provide a safer and more organized alternative to informal social media groups.

## 5. Target Users

The primary target users are:

- University students from Year 1 to Year 4.
- Students who want to save money on textbooks.
- Students who want to sell or exchange unused study materials.
- Students looking for textbooks or notes from previous semesters.

## 6. Project Scope

### In Scope

The initial version of StudySwap will include:

- Student registration and login.
- Student profile management.
- Posting textbooks and study materials for sale or exchange.
- Viewing available study materials.
- Searching and filtering listings.
- Viewing listing details.
- Contact/chat between buyers and sellers.
- Buying, selling, or exchanging study materials.
- Student verification.
- Rating and review functionality.
- Database storage for users, listings, and transactions.

### Out of Scope

The initial version will not include:

- Physical delivery services.
- Online payment processing.
- AI recommendations.
- Native mobile applications.
- Integration with universities.
- Advanced digital-content protection.

These features may be considered for future development.

## 7. Key Features

### User Management
- Register an account.
- Login and logout.
- Manage user profile.
- Student verification.

### Listing Management
- Create a listing.
- Add title, description, price, condition, and category.
- Upload images.
- Edit or delete listings.

### Search and Discovery
- Search for textbooks and study materials.
- Filter listings by category, course, price, and condition.
- View available listings.

### Communication
- Buyers can contact sellers.
- Users can communicate about price, condition, and exchange arrangements.

### Rating System
- Buyers can rate sellers.
- Users can leave reviews after completing an exchange or purchase.

## 8. Expected Benefits

StudySwap is expected to:

- Reduce students' textbook expenses.
- Make buying and selling study materials easier.
- Reduce unused textbooks and study materials.
- Save students time when searching for materials.
- Create a more organized student marketplace.

## 9. Project Deliverables

The project will deliver:

1. StudySwap website.
2. Project Charter.
3. Requirements Specification.
4. Acceptance Criteria.
5. Database Design.
6. Source code.
7. Public GitHub repository.

## 10. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if students can:

- Create and access an account.
- Create and manage study-material listings.
- Search and find relevant materials.
- Contact sellers.
- Complete a buying or exchange process.
- Rate and review other users.

The system should also be easy to understand and simple for university students to use.

## 11. Future Development

Possible future features include:

- AI-powered book recommendations.
- Mobile application.
- Online payment.
- Delivery services.
- Digital notes and study materials.
- University-specific communities.
- Course-based recommendations.
- Notifications for new listings.
- Advanced seller verification.

## 12. Project Vision

The vision of StudySwap is to become a trusted student marketplace where university students can easily exchange knowledge and study materials while saving money and reducing waste.
